Summary of Increment-4/5 Analysis 
8.1 
Quasi-steady Environment 
This section aims to summarize results from the quasi-steady acceleration analysis for this report.  
As such, it does not completely characterize the event under consideration, but serves to 
highlight the key impacts on the quasi-steady regime. 
 
For more detail, see the applicable technical narrative sections of this report.  Also, and most 
importantly, if this report does not include specific analysis for particular event that would aid in 
correlation with science or other data of interest, then please contact PIMS via email at 
pimsops@grc.nasa.gov for further assistance.
