Spacecraft Materials Microgravity Research on Flammability
PMMA flammability boundary.  The minimum in the flammability boundary is at 
very low flows, on the order of diffusive flows. This minimum occurs where the flow 
still permits enough heat release to sustain the flame but not so strong that it 
sweeps the fuel out of the hot flame zone before it can react.  As ambient O2 is 
reduced, flame temperatures are reduced, which slows down the reaction time.  
Thus the flow needed to blow off the flame becomes slower as oxygen decreases 
and the flame temperature is reduced. (Stretch rate = 3/2 U/R)
