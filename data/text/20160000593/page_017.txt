Narrow Channel Validation
All tests were opposed-flow flame spread.  The flow speed was varied one or more times 
throughout each test to see the effect on the flame.  The initial oxygen level varied between 
18% and 20%, but dropped somewhat during each test.  Video and still images of the flames 
were used to determine the flame spread rate at each flow rate and within each test.
