For large Peclet numbers (Pe>1), there is a substantial change in the non-dimensional gradient 
due to the increasing exponential nature of the temperature profiles, since heat conducted along the 
rod is convected back toward the burning tip so that heat cannot penetrate too deeply along the rod.   
For small Peclet numbers (Pe≤1), conduction dominates and the temperature profiles are nearly 
linear.  It is counter-intuitive that the slowest burning samples will, in this steady-state, have the least 
heat loss into the solid, which has been previously noted [23].
