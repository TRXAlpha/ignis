The MGR and HAR for the Saffire V PMMA burning events are shown in Figures 4 and 5. On the right axis, the 
SFU flow rate and HAR are presented while the CO2 and H2O generation rates are on the left. About the same amount 
of  CO2 is created by the one-sided sample (0.054 kg of CO2) as by the structured sample (0.0601 kg of CO2). While 
the structured PMMA is twice as thick, it does have grooves cut out of it, making it similar in combustible material as 
the one-sided PMMA.
